About

The takeaway that's been here longer than half its customers.

Galliano's has been making pizza on Lynn Road in Gaywood for more than thirty years. Independent. Family-feel. A short walk from the centre of King's Lynn, on the eastern side of town, with a steady rotation of regulars who already know what they want before they ring.

The dough is prepared from scratch on the premises every day before service. The cheese sits all the way out to the edge of the base — the corner crust toasts into something close to a cheese-on-toast, which is the bit a lot of regulars actually come back for.

Deep pan as standard, in four sizes from a personal seven-inch up to a fourteen-inch sharer. Six signature pizzas, a wrap, a pie, a calzone, and a gluten-free base option that's prepared with the same care. Side orders, pasta, bakes, kids' menu, meal deals, desserts. Nothing on the menu is trying to be clever. It's all trying to be good.
